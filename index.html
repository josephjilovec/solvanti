01 / Start with the household

What do you need your energy to do?

Lower routine consumption. Keep a few essentials running during an outage. Prepare for an electric vehicle. These are different goals, with different costs and design questions. A product quote rarely answers all of them on its own.

Solvanti’s proposed starting point is a human-reviewed household energy brief. It organizes bill history, priorities and property questions before a homeowner chooses equipment, financing or an installer.

04 / A practical path

From an open question
to an informed next step.

  1. Define your priority

    Choose the decision you are trying to make and the household activities that matter. Collect the bill periods and property context relevant to that decision.

  2. Compare on a consistent basis

    Record the scope and assumptions behind each option: included work, operating limitations, maintenance and costs that still need confirmation.

  3. Get a qualified design

    Take the brief to the appropriate assessor or licensed contractor for site evaluation, technical sizing, permitting and a written proposal.

07 / Good questions

A little clarity
before we talk.

Can you quote or install a system?

Solvanti is currently a planning concept. Equipment sales, electrical design, installation and service contracts are not available through this page.

Will you calculate guaranteed savings?

No. Any later scenario tool would need verified inputs and transparent assumptions. Actual economics depend on the property, usage, rates, equipment and quoted work.

Do you advise on incentives or financing?

The brief can record questions to verify with relevant professionals and current program sources. This website does not quote incentive amounts, financing terms or eligibility.

Do I need to share utility credentials?

No. An initial inquiry only needs a general goal and region. Never send login credentials or full account identifiers. Document handling would be agreed before a pilot.
